About us

HCMUTE Developer Student Club (HCMUTE DSC) is an open, active academic club for every student passionate about technology and programming, under the Faculty of Information Technology, Ho Chi Minh City University of Technology and Engineering.

Our 2021 introduction video, back when the club was called GDSC HCMUTE.

Our journey

  1. 2021

    Founded as GDSC HCMUTE

    Google Developer Student Clubs – HCMUTE is founded at the Faculty of Information Technology.

  2. Oct 10, 2021

    Club introduction video

    The first introduction video of the club is released.

  3. Aug 2022

    First HCMUTE CTF

    The club runs the first "Students and Information Security" contest (HCMUTE CTF).

  4. 2023

    Git & GitHub workshop, GDSC Vietnam Hackathon

    First Git & GitHub workshop, GDSC Vietnam Hackathon 2023 and the launch of the club website.

  5. Feb–Apr 2024

    Git & GitHub Study Jam

    A team-based Git & GitHub learning program for IT students, sponsored by KMS Healthcare.

  6. Oct 2024

    Becoming HCMUTE Developer Student Club

    Leaving the Google Developer Student Clubs program, the club becomes HCMUTE Developer Student Club (HCMUTE DSC) with a new direction and identity, and celebrates its 3rd anniversary.

  7. 2025

    SQL Challenge & Git & GitHub Study Jam 2025

    Learning Sprint SQL Challenge, a workshop on labour rights in IT, and Git & GitHub Study Jam 2025 with products from every team.

  8. 2026

    InterviewPrep101, ClubDay & Gen 7

    The InterviewPrep101 series, SQL Challenge 2026, Gen 7 recruitment, ClubDay and the new website at hcmute-dsc.io.vn.
